How these numbers work+

One defense. One position. One game.

We add every supplied player’s PPR points against a defense for that position and week. FPA per game is that total divided by the number of observed games. Zero and negative scores are included. A missing position or week stays unavailable.

The opponent determines the defense.

vs TB means the player was home and Tampa Bay’s defense was away. @ NYG means the player was away and the Giants’ defense was home. Venue filters always describe the defense.

Ranks use the actual averages.

Rank 1 allows the fewest points. Higher ranks are easier scoring matchups. We rank unrounded averages; ties share a rank. The league average weights each observed defense game equally. Player ranks in the source are not defense ranks.

Only the supplied 2026 results.

Recent form compares the selected recent window with the season so far. Those samples overlap. Rows without an opponent cannot be assigned to a defense. Early-season results describe the sample; they do not project a player’s next score.
