Preparing the opponent-adjusted rankings…

The colored bar is actual FPA/game. The pale track and white tick are the expected scoring of the offenses faced. Rank 1 allowed the smallest share of expected points. Right-hand badges show suppression and opponent offense strength; a positive offense percentage means a stronger-than-average schedule.

How opponent-adjusted toughness is calculated

Suppression = 100 × (1 − actual total ÷ expected total). Expected scoring adds each opponent’s supplied TSUMS positional average once per game. Higher suppression receives a tougher rank, so low raw FPA against weak offenses does not automatically rank first.

Opponent offense strength = 100 × (expected FPA/game ÷ league offense average − 1). The league baseline weights the supplied TSUMS positional averages by games. Actual and expected scoring share the selected position and defense venue.

These are comparisons from the supplied three-week sample. Offense baselines include games against the selected defense. Missing or nonpositive baselines remain unavailable.

Preparing the aligned matchup scatter…

Each defense label has one spoke. All of its games lie exactly on that spoke; radius shows the actual FPA allowed in that game. Smaller logo markers retain the original scatter’s colors. Hover any dot for its offense, week, score, and the other games on that spoke. Equal scores occupy the same location. Scroll horizontally to inspect the full-size chart.

How expected FPA works

Expected: we add the TSUMS position average of each opponent offense once per observed game. ALL uses the supplied ALLx average. Actual: we add the player points scored against the defense in FPAv2, including zero and negative results.

Both scatter axes use totals over the same games. The rank view ranks those totals from lowest (1) to highest. Offense ranks in the opponent table come directly from TSUMS, where 1 means the most offensive points. The baseline includes the offenses’ results against the selected defense.

Venue describes the defense: a player’s vs TB puts TB away; @ NYG puts NYG at home. Missing opponent baselines remain unavailable. Player search and hiding zeros affect the player list only.
