03INDIVIDUAL MATCHUPS / POLAR SCATTER

Every game, on its defense’s spoke

Preparing the aligned matchup scatter…

Each defense label has one spoke. All of its games lie exactly on that spoke; radius shows the actual FPA allowed in that game. Each small logo identifies the opposing offense that scored those points; its ring retains the original scatter’s colors. Hover any dot for its offense, week, score, and the other games on that spoke. Equal scores occupy the same location. Scroll horizontally to inspect the full-size chart.

How expected FPA works

Expected: we add the TSUMS position average of each opponent offense once per observed game. ALL uses the supplied ALLx average. Actual: we add the player points scored against the defense in FPAv2, including zero and negative results.

Both scatter axes use totals over the same games. The rank view ranks those totals from lowest (1) to highest. Offense ranks in the opponent table come directly from TSUMS, where 1 means the most offensive points. The baseline includes the offenses’ results against the selected defense.

Venue describes the defense: a player’s vs TB puts TB away; @ NYG puts NYG at home. Missing opponent baselines remain unavailable. Player search and hiding zeros affect the player list only.
